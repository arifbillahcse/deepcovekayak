Questions?

Frequently Asked

Do we need any paddling experience?

Not at all. Our programs are beginner-friendly and led by certified instructors who provide a full safety briefing and hands-on guidance before you set off.

What's the minimum and maximum group size?

We accommodate groups of most sizes — from small teams to large corporate events. Share your numbers in the inquiry form and we'll confirm availability and the best setup for you.

What should we bring and wear?

Wear quick-dry clothing and bring a change of clothes, water, and sunscreen. We provide kayaks/boards, paddles, PFDs, safety gear, and wetsuits if needed.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Safety comes first. If conditions aren't suitable, our team will work with you to reschedule to another available date at no penalty.

How far in advance should we book?

Summer weekends fill up quickly, so we recommend inquiring as early as possible. Final participant numbers and full payment are due 3 days before your activity.
